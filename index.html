07 Questions

Straight answers

How does the 2-week trial work?

For your first 2 weeks I set up and run your Meta ads with no management fee. The only cost is your ad spend, which you pay directly to Meta on your own card — I never touch it. You see every lead as it comes in.

What happens after the 2 weeks?

You decide. If you like the results, we keep going month to month at the rate we agree on during our first call. If not, you stop — no contract, no cancellation fee, and every lead stays yours.

How much should I spend on ads?

Depends on your market, your average job size and how many jobs you can handle. We set a starting budget together on the first call — one you're comfortable with — and scale from there based on real results.

Do I need a website to run ads?

You don't need one to start — Meta's instant lead forms let people request a quote without leaving Facebook or Instagram. But your ads will perform better with a solid website. Most homeowners look you up before they call, and a clean, fast site with real photos and reviews turns that quick check into a booked job. A site also lets Meta's tracking learn from your visitors, so it gets better at finding the right people. That's why I build them as an add-on.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. Ads run in your business's own account, paid on your card. I get partner access to manage it, and you can remove that access any time.

How fast will I see leads?

Ads usually start delivering within days of launch. The first few weeks are for testing creative and offers, then we push budget behind what's working.

Why Meta instead of Google?

Google catches people who are already searching. Meta puts your work in front of homeowners before they search — perfect for visual jobs like epoxy floors, roofs, paint and clean driveways. Pair it with one of my websites and you cover both: every site I build is set up for local SEO, so it works to show up when people in your area search Google for your service. Meta creates the demand, your site catches the searches — Google-style reach without paying for every click.
